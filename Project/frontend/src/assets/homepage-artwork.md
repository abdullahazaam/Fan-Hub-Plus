# Homepage environment artwork

These are original decorative environments generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. They are used only while an existing API image is loading or unavailable, and are labeled as universe illustrations rather than franchise or character artwork. Successful original images remain authoritative.

Create an original cinematic environment painting for an editorial fandom homepage. Wide landscape composition, no text, no logos, no UI. A monumental futuristic city at night, densely layered blackened steel towers and intricate suspended bridges over a deep urban avenue, moody near-black and charcoal palette, selective deep crimson architectural lighting reflected on rain-wet stone, a subtle warm ivory window light here and there. Premium highly detailed film production design, physically convincing stone and metal, controlled studio-like contrasts, atmospheric depth, realistic cinematic materials, an original cyberpunk metropolis with no recognizable existing franchise landmarks. Human-scale tiny anonymous silhouettes for scale. A broad architectural focal point on the left and layered depth across the entire frame. No purple, pink, blue or cyan lighting, no neon advertisements, no visible lettering, no wireframe, no particles, no workstation, no desks. The mood is luxurious, mysterious and immersive, not a simple vector illustration.
